Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Countersign is a sole-trader business (ABN [ABN]) based in Mango Hill, Queensland. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what we do with it. "We" and "us" mean Countersign.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, pharmacy name, email, phone number and your message.
  • Bookings: the details you enter when you book a call, such as your name, email and preferred time.
  • Payments: your name, email, the amount paid and whether payment went through. Card details are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • While we work together: business details and documents you choose to share, such as policies, registers and training records.

Patient health information

We don’t want or accept patient health information. Please don’t send it to us through the form, by email or in shared folders. If we receive any by mistake, we will delete it and let you know.

How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • reply to your enquiry
  • book and deliver the services you ask for
  • send invoices and keep business and tax records.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t add you to marketing lists.

Where it is stored

We use a small number of trusted providers. Some of them may store information outside Australia.

  • Formspree — receives messages sent through the contact form.
  • Stripe — processes payments.
  • [Booking tool, e.g. Cal.com or Calendly] — manages call bookings.
  • [Email and file-sharing provider, e.g. Google Workspace or Microsoft 365] — email and shared folders.

Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies, and doesn’t use Google Analytics. The Stripe and booking pages we link to are run by those providers and follow their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to work with you. Some records, such as invoices, must be kept for five years for tax purposes.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. We will delete it on request, unless the law requires us to keep it (for example, tax records).

Questions or complaints

Email [EMAIL]. We will reply as soon as we can. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
