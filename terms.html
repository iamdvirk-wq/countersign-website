1. Our services

QSPP Gap Check ($249): a 45-minute video call reviewing your pharmacy’s current accreditation setup, followed by a written gap report within 5 business days of the call. The report sets out what’s ready, what’s missing and what to fix first.

Readiness Sprint ($1,200): 2–3 weeks of remote work to organise your evidence register by QSPP domain, set up policy version control, set up your training and refresher, incident, complaints and feedback registers, and prepare an assessment-day pack. We will confirm the start date and timeline with you in writing before we begin.

Care plan ($199 per month): a monthly evidence top-up, training-expiry reminders, preparation for your mid-cycle review, and a quarterly check of your program claim records.

All services are delivered remotely, by video call, email and shared folders. Prices are in Australian dollars.

Anything clinical must be reviewed and signed off by your pharmacist. Your pharmacy stays responsible for its clinical practice, its accreditation, its claims under government-funded programs, and its compliance with the law and with the accreditation standard.
