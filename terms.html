QSPP Gap Check terms

Last updated: October 2026

These terms apply when you book a QSPP Gap Check with Countersign (ABN [ABN]). By paying for a Gap Check, you agree to them.

1. What’s included

  • A 45-minute video call reviewing your pharmacy’s current accreditation setup.
  • A written gap report within 5 business days of the call, showing what’s ready, what’s missing and what to fix first.

The Gap Check is done remotely. The fee is $249 (Australian dollars), paid when you book.

2. What’s not included

  • Clinical advice.
  • Writing clinical policies.
  • Site visits.
  • Handling medicines or patient records.

Anything clinical is reviewed and signed off by your pharmacist. Please don’t share any patient information with us.

3. No guarantee of outcome

Accreditation decisions are made by the assessor, not by Countersign. We can’t guarantee your pharmacy will pass. The report is based on the information you show us, and is a guide to help you get your evidence organised and complete.

4. Confidentiality

We keep your business information confidential. We use it only to deliver the Gap Check and any follow-on work you book. We don’t share it with anyone else unless you agree or the law requires it. You can ask us to delete documents you’ve shared once the work is finished. See our privacy policy for more.

5. Refunds

You get a full refund if:

  • you cancel before the call, or
  • your report isn’t delivered within 10 business days of the call.

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

6. Credit towards a Readiness Sprint

If you book a Readiness Sprint within 30 days of your Gap Check call, the $249 Gap Check fee comes off the Sprint price.
